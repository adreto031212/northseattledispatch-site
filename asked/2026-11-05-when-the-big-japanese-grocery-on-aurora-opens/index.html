When the big Japanese grocery on Aurora opens

November 5, 2026 · North Seattle Dispatch, issue 4 · Every answered question

A reader asked when the big Japanese grocery on Aurora opens. The store is Hashi Market, moving into a 35,715-square-foot space at 13025 Aurora Avenue N, a strip mall that was the former Amazon Fresh store. The report, from October 6, does not give an opening date, and we found none published since. Check the store's own channels before you plan a trip. KIRO 7
