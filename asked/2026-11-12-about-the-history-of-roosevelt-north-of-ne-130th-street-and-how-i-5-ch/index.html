About the history of Roosevelt north of NE 130th Street, and how I-5 changed it

November 12, 2026 · North Seattle Dispatch, issue 5 · Every answered question

A reader asked about the history of Roosevelt north of NE 130th Street, and how I-5 changed it. HistoryLink's essay on the freeway's route says the state took a right-of-way two to three blocks wide through North Seattle, bent to keep the road on the west edge of Northgate Mall. Its essay on the Everett leg says I-5 opened from Everett to the Seattle city limit at NE 145th Street on February 3, 1965; before that, the way north was Highway 99, with 26 traffic lights between the city limits. Neither essay covers Roosevelt Way's own blocks, so we cannot yet say which side streets the freeway cut. The newest chapter is on the map: Pinehurst Station opened beside I-5 at NE 130th Street on September 30. HistoryLink: the route · HistoryLink: the 1965 opening · Sound Transit
