The I-5 southbound on-ramp at NE 130th Street closes around the clock for about 14 days, starting as soon as Monday, October 19

October 17, 2026 · North Seattle Dispatch, issue 1 · All North Seattle news

SDOT needs it for demolition and concrete work on the NE 130th and NE 125th Street project. Drivers headed south are detoured to the on-ramps at NE Northgate Way and Corliss Ave N, by Aurora Ave N or by 15th Ave NE and Pinehurst Way NE. SDOT says the dates can change. SDOT
