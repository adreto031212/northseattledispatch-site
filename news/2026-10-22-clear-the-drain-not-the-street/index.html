North Seattle · One useful thing

clear the drain, not the street

October 22, 2026 · North Seattle Dispatch, issue 2 · All North Seattle news

Leaves on a storm grate back water up onto the street and toward basements once the November rain starts. Seattle Public Utilities asks you to clear only the top of the grate, standing on the curb, with a rake or broom, never bare hands and never on a major arterial. Leaves go in the yard waste cart or paper bags; in November SPU collects 10 extra bags free. Still plugged? Call (206) 386-1800. Adopt a drain near you, and SPU lends rakes and leaf claws.
