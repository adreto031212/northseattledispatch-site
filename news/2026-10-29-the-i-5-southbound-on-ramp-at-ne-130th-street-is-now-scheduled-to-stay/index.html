The I-5 southbound on-ramp at NE 130th Street is now scheduled to stay closed until 5 AM Thursday, November 5

October 29, 2026 · North Seattle Dispatch, issue 3 · All North Seattle news

SDOT's schedule has it closed around the clock from 5 AM Monday, October 26, for demolition and concrete work. Drivers headed south detour to the on-ramps at NE Northgate Way and Corliss Ave N, by Aurora Ave N or by 15th Ave NE and Pinehurst Way NE. SDOT says the dates can change. SDOT
