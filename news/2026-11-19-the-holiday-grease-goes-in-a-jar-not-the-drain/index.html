North Seattle · One useful thing

the holiday grease goes in a jar, not the drain

November 19, 2026 · North Seattle Dispatch, issue 6 · All North Seattle news

Cooking oil and grease build up in pipes and cause clogs, and Seattle Public Utilities says running hot water, vinegar or soap after them does not help. Let the grease cool first. Wipe small amounts out of the pan with a paper towel and put them in the food and yard waste cart; pour larger amounts into a sealable container, like a milk carton or a jar, and put it in the garbage. The same advice keeps grease out of creeks like Pipers, where the chum are spawning now. Seattle Public Utilities
