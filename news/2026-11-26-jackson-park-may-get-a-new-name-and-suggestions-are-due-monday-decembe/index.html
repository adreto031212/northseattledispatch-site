Jackson Park may get a new name, and suggestions are due Monday, December 14

November 26, 2026 · North Seattle Dispatch, issue 7 · All North Seattle news

Mayor Katie Wilson has directed Seattle Parks to start a naming process for the park at 1000 NE 135th Street, named for President Andrew Jackson, whose policies forced Native peoples from their lands. Email a name and a short note on how it fits the city's criteria (the land's history, its natural features, its location, or its historical or cultural significance) to PKS_BPRC@seattle.gov. The Parks Naming Committee recommends a name and the Parks superintendent decides. A survey on how you use the park and its trails comes next, with QR-code signs in the park. Seattle Parks
