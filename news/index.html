October 29, 2026

The I-5 southbound on-ramp at NE 130th Street is now scheduled to stay closed until 5 AM Thursday, November 5

SDOT's schedule has it closed around the clock from 5 AM Monday, October 26, for demolition and concrete work.

Ballots for the November 3 general election are due by 8 PM Tuesday

King County's 100 drop boxes close at 8 PM sharp on Election Day, and a mailed ballot must be postmarked by Election Day to count.

Tonight and Friday night, from 11 PM, light rail runs on one track at Pinehurst and Shoreline South/148th

Expect delays of up to 20 minutes toward Lynnwood City Center, and 2 Line trains turn back at Northgate.

The council's budget package comes out Tuesday, November 3, and the public hearing is Thursday, November 5

The budget committee takes the package up Wednesday at 9:30 AM. At the hearing, remote comment starts at 1 PM and in-person comment at 5 PM; written comment to councilmembers is open any time.

You can ask for a public EV charger at the curb near where you live

Seattle City Light and SDOT are adding up to 200 curbside charging locations; applications stay open through fall 2027, and installation runs from fall 2027 through 2030.

new batteries when the clocks change

October 22, 2026

The I-5 southbound on-ramp at NE 130th Street now closes as soon as Monday, October 26, a week later than first scheduled

The closure is around the clock for about 14 days of demolition and concrete work.

The new roundabout at Roosevelt Way NE and 10th Ave NE is open to vehicles in all directions

It has flashing crossing beacons and curb-protected bike lanes; SDOT's project page shows how to walk, bike and drive through it.

Several elevators at Pinehurst Station have been out since Monday, October 19

For the Lynnwood platform, go to the south end of the plaza and take elevator 1; for the Federal Way and Downtown Redmond platform, elevator 2.

Carkeek Park's free salmon weekends start Saturday, October 24

Creekside Salmon Journey Days run Saturdays and Sundays through December 6, 10:30 AM to 3:30 PM, on Piper's Creek.

Three free Halloween events at North Seattle community centers

Northgate's family pumpkin carving is Friday, October 23, 4:30 to 6:30 PM (not Monday, as issue one had it).

Seattle's 2027–28 budget: public hearing November 5, final vote November 20

The council publishes its balancing package November 3.
