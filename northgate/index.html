The latest in Northgate

November 12, 2026

The Northgate Station parking garage has fewer spaces on weekdays from 7 AM to 4 PM, from November 3 through January 29, while construction closes parts of levels B3 and B4. Station access continues during the work. Sound Transit SOLD:Northgate

November 5, 2026

The John Lewis Memorial Bridge opened on October 2, 2021. It stretches about 1,900 feet over I-5, from North Seattle College at College Way N and N 100th Street to 1st Ave NE and NE 100th Street, near Northgate Station. On the east side, a two-way protected bike lane runs along 1st Ave NE from NE 92nd to NE 103rd Street. SDOT

October 29, 2026

The John Lewis Memorial Bridge turned five on October 2. It runs about 1,900 feet over I-5, from North Seattle College at College Way N and N 100th Street to 1st Ave NE and NE 100th Street, near Northgate Station. On the east side, a two-way protected bike lane runs along 1st Ave NE from NE 92nd to NE 103rd Street. SDOT

October 22, 2026

Hubbard Homestead Park, 11203 5th Ave NE, is named for Harry Hubbard's family, who homesteaded the site from 1913 to 1968. Its "wet meadow" filters runoff before it reaches Thornton Creek, and a spring runnel crosses the park diagonally, with the artworks "Mnemonic Spring" and "Cloud Chamber". Pumpkin carving at the community center is Friday, October 23. Hubbard Homestead

October 17, 2026

Northgate Community Center, 10510 5th Ave NE, holds a free pumpkin carving on Monday, October 19, 4:30 to 6:30 PM; the center shares its parking lot with the Northgate Library just to the north. For the next two weeks the NE Northgate Way on-ramp is one of the two detours for drivers who would use the closed NE 130th Street on-ramp to I-5 south. Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a North Seattle newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in North Seattle that is news too.

Who writes it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publisher. Every item comes from North Seattle's own public record (council agendas, permit notices, school district and transit updates, parks calendars) and links its source. It is researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

How often, and is it really free?

Once a week, Thursday at 6 AM, about five minutes to read. Free, no paywall, unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publishing company. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It is shared with a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher, which may contact you about your home, and is never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
