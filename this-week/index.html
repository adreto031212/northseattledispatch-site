This week in North Seattle.

The dated things to know between now and next Thursday, from issue 7 of the North Seattle Dispatch. Every item links its public source.

Heads up

  • Seattle's shoreline, late Wednesday night through Thursday afternoon: coastal flood watch. As of Tuesday morning, the National Weather Service says significant coastal flooding is possible Thursday, with water up to 2.5 to 3.5 feet above ground in low-lying spots, and some roads may close. Allow extra time, and never drive around barricades or through water of unknown depth. National Weather Service
  • Thursday and Friday, November 26 and 27: Link runs a Sunday schedule Thursday and a Saturday schedule Friday. Metro buses run a Sunday schedule Thursday. Sound Transit · King County Metro
  • I-5 near NE 130th Street, Monday to Thursday nights, November 30 to December 3, 9 PM to 5 AM: overnight lane closures. Southbound lanes close Monday and Tuesday nights and northbound lanes Wednesday and Thursday nights, with at least one lane open. The northbound on-ramp from NE Northgate Way closes Wednesday and Thursday nights: take the southbound on-ramp, exit at Corliss Ave N, then use the northbound on-ramp from 1st Ave NE. SDOT

What the city decided and what changed

Jackson Park may get a new name, and suggestions are due Monday, December 14

Mayor Katie Wilson has directed Seattle Parks to start a naming process for the park at 1000 NE 135th Street, named for President Andrew Jackson, whose policies forced Native peoples from their lands.

The City Council passed Seattle's 2027 budget 7-2 on Friday, November 20

Councilmembers Rinck and Rivera voted no.

Every Seattle Public Library branch is closed Thursday, and the city's community centers, teen centers and indoor pools are closed Thursday and Friday

Parks stay open on their usual hours both days, and Jackson Park Golf Course runs 7 AM to 2 PM. Seattle Public Library · Seattle Parks
