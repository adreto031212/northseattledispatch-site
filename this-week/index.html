This week in North Seattle.

The dated things to know between now and next Thursday, from issue 5 of the North Seattle Dispatch. Every item links its public source.

Heads up

  • I-5 southbound on-ramp at NE 130th Street: closed around the clock until as soon as 5 AM Friday, November 13. Use the on-ramps at NE Northgate Way or Corliss Ave N. More below. SDOT
  • I-5 northbound on-ramp from NE Northgate Way: closed tonight and Friday night, November 12 and 13, from as soon as 9 PM to 5 AM. SDOT points drivers to the northbound on-ramp on 1st Ave NE; check its detour map before you go. SDOT
  • 2 Line: no trains north of International District/Chinatown Station, Saturday and Sunday, November 14 and 15. From Pinehurst and Northgate, ride the 1 Line, which runs every 8 minutes. Sound Transit

What the city decided and what changed

The I-5 southbound on-ramp at NE 130th Street did not reopen for good on November 5: it is closed again until as soon as 5 AM Friday, November 13

SDOT's November 6 update opened the ramp for the weekend from as soon as 5 PM Friday, November 6, and restarted the around-the-clock closure as soon as 5 AM Monday, November 9.

The council's committee votes on the 2027 budget start Friday, November 13, at 9:30 AM, and the final vote is set for Friday, November 20, at 1 PM

After last Thursday's public hearing, the council's calendar set this week aside for amendments, with more committee votes on November 16 and 19.

The city has posted a draft vision for the North Aurora corridor, N 80th to N 145th Streets, and is taking comments on it online
